Te Papa Tongarewa · A photographic journey, c.1875

James Bragge — Wellington to the Manawatū

In the mid-1870s the Wellington photographer James Bragge loaded his wet-plate camera onto a horse-drawn van and worked his way up the new coach road from Wellington, over the Remutaka (Rimutaka) Range and across the Wairarapa, into the Forty- and Seventy-Mile Bush and on through the Manawatū Gorge. The resulting album, “New Zealand scenery: Wellington to the Wairarapa”, is one of the country’s great early topographical records.
